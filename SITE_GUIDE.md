## How to use it

### Lessons

Each chapter shows short key points and a Madina's Mart example, which is one shop business followed through the whole course. Tap **Explain more** for the full explanation and the **Also tested in the exam** notes. Every chapter ends with key terms and a button to practise it.

### Practice

- **Practise a chapter**: from the chapter page or the Practice page.
- **Weak points**: chapters ordered weakest first. Untried chapters come first, then your lowest scores.
- **What I have studied**: a mixed set from every chapter you have practised.
- **Section B cases**: each case has a scenario and several tasks.
- **Modes**: *One at a time* checks each answer straight away. *All at once* marks everything when you submit.
- **Number answers**: type them as you like, for example `1,250`, `4,58`, `23.58%`, `1.35:1`, `90 days` or `15.82 times`. A comma before one or two final digits is read as a decimal point.
- Your answers survive a page reload in the middle of a set.

### Mock exam

- 35 objective test questions worth 2 marks each, drawn from every chapter in proportion to its size.
- 2 case questions worth 15 marks each: one single-company or cash-flow case, and one group case.
- 100 marks, a 2-hour timer and a pass mark of 50%. Every paper is different.
- The timer keeps running if you leave the page. When time runs out, the paper is marked automatically.
- After you submit you get your mark, pass or not passed, your score by chapter, and every question marked with an explanation. The result is kept after a reload.

### English exam terms in Russian and Uzbek

The exam is in English and uses one official term, but the Russian and Uzbek lessons use a translated word. So that the two always match, the official English term is shown as a small tag next to the translated word, for example *Дебиторы* `receivables` or *rezerv* `allowance`.

- It appears in lessons, key points, exam notes, key terms and answer explanations.
- Each term is tagged once per block of text, so the page stays readable.
- It is tagged only when the English version of the same text uses that term. A word with several meanings (such as "резерв", which can mean allowance, provision or reserve) therefore gets the right English term.
- The key terms list shows the English term next to the translation.

### Terms page (same thing, different name)

The **Terms** page lists about 55 exam terms with the other names the Kaplan books, your teacher's slides and older textbooks use for the same thing, for example:

| Official exam term | Other names |
|---|---|
| statement of financial position | SFP, SOFP, balance sheet (older name) |
| statement of cash flows | cash flow statement, SCF |
| statement of changes in equity | SOCE, SOCIE |
| carrying amount | net book value, carrying value |
| irrecoverable debts | bad debts |
| allowance for receivables | allowance for doubtful debts |
| returns inwards / outwards | sales returns / purchase returns |
| profit for the year | net profit |
| operating profit | profit before financing and income taxes (PBFIT), trading profit |
| loan notes | loan stock, debentures |

A dashed name is an older or informal one that the Kaplan books do not use. Look-alike terms that are different carry their formula, for example *gearing* (debt ÷ (equity + debt)) and *debt/equity ratio* (debt ÷ equity), which the Kit asks separately in Q517 and Q518, and *mark-up* (profit ÷ cost) against *gross profit margin* (profit ÷ sales). You can search any name in English, Russian or Uzbek.

## Design

- **Name**: Balanced.
- **Theme**: one theme, "Clean", with two modes. Light is paper, ink and one blue. Dark is navy, orange and a warm glow. You asked for the dark mode to stay exactly as it is, so it is not to be restyled without your say.
- **Your own Naruto theme later**: the site is set up for a second theme. To add it:
  1. Add `'naruto'` to `THEMES` in `js/theme.js`.
  2. Write its styles under `[data-theme="naruto"]` in `style.css`.
  3. Add a way to pick it, for example a second button in the header.
- **Motion**: pages and answers animate gently. The animation switches off if your device is set to reduce motion.
- **History**: the first designs used a Naruto look with a dojo name, a drawn scene, kanji and rank wording. You rejected them as not looking like Naruto and cringe, so all of that was removed. The orange dark mode is what was kept.

## Other ACCA papers

The Kaplan books cover FA (FFA) only. They contain nothing for F7, which is now called Financial Reporting (FR). For FR you would need Kaplan's FR Study Text and Exam Kit, and the site could then take an FR course alongside FA.

### Rules

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no framework and no build step. It must open from `file://`, so use plain `<script>` tags, not modules.
- All content goes through `el()` in `js/dom.js`, never `innerHTML`.
- Every label needs English, Russian and Uzbek.
- Run the tests with `node --test "tests/*.test.mjs"`. There are 324, all passing. Never run `node --test tests/`, which fails on Node 24.
- The tests check that every Kit question 1-550 is covered exactly once, that every case totals 15 marks, that key points stay short and match across languages, that the Terms page translations match the chapter glossaries, and that every browser script compiles.

### How to add things

- **A term to tag**: add an entry to `TERMS` in `js/terms.js` with its English, Russian and Uzbek patterns. `*` matches any further letters.
- **A name on the Terms page**: add an entry to `data/term-names.js`. A trailing `*` on a name marks it as older or informal.
- **A chapter**: add `data/chapter-NN.js` and `data/questions-chNN.js`, list both in `index.html`, and register the questions in `tests/content.test.mjs`.
- **A theme**: see Design above.

### Updating the phone link

After changing the site, republish the page to the same link. The publish copy is the body of `index.html` without its `<html>`, `<head>` and `<body>` wrappers, plus `style.css` and every file under `data/` and `js/`.
